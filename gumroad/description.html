SFX Manager

Premium sound-FX panel for After Effects & Premiere Pro

Stop digging through folders mid-edit. SFX Manager turns your panel into a fast, beautiful sound library: your disk structure, instant waveform preview, and one click to drop the sound exactly at the playhead.

What you get

  • Folder tree = your disk — point it at any SFX folder; the structure comes straight across
  • Waveform preview — Web Audio playback with volume & speed control before you commit
  • Add at playhead — one click places the clip on the timeline in AE and Premiere Pro
  • Auto-preview — selecting a sound just plays it, no extra clicks
  • Drag to timeline — drag a sound straight from the panel
  • Favorites, recents & color tags — find sounds the way you think
  • Zoom + list/grid views — fits tall, narrow docks too
  • Dark & light themes — flat premium UI with a customizable accent color
  • Signed ZXP — installs with any standard ZXP installer

Requirements

  • Adobe After Effects or Premiere Pro (CC 2020 or newer)
  • Windows or macOS

Install — about 2 minutes

  1. Download the .zxp file from this purchase
  2. Install it with any ZXP installer (Anastasiy's Extension Manager, ExManCmd, …)
  3. Restart After Effects / Premiere Pro → Window → Extensions → SFX Manager
